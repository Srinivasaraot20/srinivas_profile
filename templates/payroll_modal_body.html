Project Overview

Smart Payroll System is an enterprise-grade HR and Payroll platform that helps organizations manage their workforce efficiently. The system provides centralized employee records, automated payroll calculations, attendance monitoring, compliance reporting, and detailed analytics through an intuitive web-based dashboard.

The platform is suitable for startups, SMEs, educational institutions, agencies, and enterprise organizations seeking a complete workforce management solution.

The system employs a strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) model to ensure security and proper delegation of responsibilities.

Super Admin
Full Access

Complete system configuration access, user administration, corporate structures configuration, security controls definition, and log auditing.

HR Manager
Operations

Workforce onboarding workflows, department assignments, compliance management, leave scheduling approval, and operational reports access.

Payroll Manager
Financials

Automated payroll cycles processing, salary structures modifications, allowances/deductions management, payslip distribution, and payroll reporting.

Department Manager
Team Lead

Department shift setups, attendance validation and corrections, team leave requests approval, and team performance tracking.

Employee
Self-Service

Access to self-service portal, check leave balances, submit leave applications, download payslips, register shift check-ins/outs, and update basic credentials.
