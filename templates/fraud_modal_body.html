Project Overview

Health insurance fraud is a major challenge in modern healthcare systems, resulting in significant financial losses and delays in claim processing. This project presents an intelligent and explainable AI-powered health insurance claim verification system that automates fraud detection using advanced Artificial Intelligence, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques.

The system extracts key medical and insurance-related information from uploaded claim documents using OCR. BioGPT embeddings and a Transformer-based RoBERTa classifier are utilized to analyze claim data and classify claims as legitimate or fraudulent. To address class imbalance, a hybrid sampling approach combining SMOTE and Random Undersampling is applied.

For transparency and trust, SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations) is integrated to explain model decisions and highlight the factors influencing fraud predictions. An AI-powered chatbot provides real-time responses to claim-related queries and retrieves updated policy information to assist users throughout the verification process.
